Privacy Policy — Hookfall

Effective date: 3 September 2026 · Updated 8 October 2026: the Game, previously published as Abyss Angler, is now named Hookfall — its data practices are unchanged · App: Hookfall (com.plsstudios.abyssangle) · Developer: PLS Studios

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play Hookfall (the "Game") and how it is used. By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect directly

None. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any account. Your game progress (coins, levels, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us. We do not operate any servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free to play and is supported by advertising. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google and its certified ad partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, which can include:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, screen size, language)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped) and diagnostic data

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from sites and apps that use its services
  • List of AdMob's certified ad partners

3. Your advertising choices

You can limit or reset ad tracking at any time in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can delete or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you may decline.

4. Children

The Game is a general-audience app and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads served in the Game follow Google's advertising policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help address it.

5. Data retention and security

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any server of ours, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Game progress stored on your device can be removed at any time by using "Reset all progress" in the Game's settings or by uninstalling the app. Data collected by Google is retained and secured according to Google's policies linked above.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or certain US states such as California), you may have legal rights over personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Because we do not hold any personal data about you ourselves, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device (resetting or deleting your Advertising ID, or uninstalling the app) or with Google using the links in section 2. You can also contact us at the address below and we will help direct your request. We will never sell your personal information.

7. International data transfers

Data collected by Google for advertising may be processed on servers located outside your own country. Google safeguards such transfers using mechanisms recognized by applicable law (such as Standard Contractual Clauses), as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices change (for example if we add analytics or online features), this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before the change takes effect.
